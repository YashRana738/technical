## 1. Technical Writing — Definition + Key Points (Most likely 10-mark or 5-mark)

**Definition:** Technical writing is a type of writing that communicates complex technical information in a clear, concise, and structured way to a specific audience.

**Key characteristics (memorize these 6):**
1. **Clear** — no confusion
2. **Concise** — no extra words
3. **Accurate** — correct facts and data
4. **Objective** — no personal opinions
5. **Structured** — uses headings, lists, visuals
6. **Audience-focused** — written for a specific reader

**One-liner to remember:** *"Technical writing = clear facts for the right audience."*

---

## 2. Thesis Components — Structure (Most likely 10-mark)

Just remember this order:

> **Title → Abstract → Acknowledgement → Table of Contents → Introduction → Literature Review → Methodology → Results → Discussion → Conclusion → References → Appendix**

**Memory trick:** *"T-A-A-T-I-L-M-R-D-C-R-A"* — or just remember the flow:
- First 4 = front matter (title, abstract, acknowledgement, TOC)
- Next 3 = setup (intro, lit review, methodology)
- Next 3 = core (results, discussion, conclusion)
- Last 2 = back matter (references, appendix)

---

## 3. Abstract vs Introduction (Most likely 5-mark)

| | Abstract | Introduction |
|---|---|---|
| **Length** | 150–300 words | 1–3 pages |
| **Content** | Summary of entire paper | Background + problem + objectives |
| **Standalone?** | Yes | No |
| **When written?** | Last | First |

**One-liner:** *"Abstract = short summary of everything. Introduction = detailed background."*

---

## 4. Literature Review + Research Gap (Most likely 5-mark)

- **Literature Review** = Summary of what others have studied on your topic.
- **Research Gap** = What has NOT been studied yet.
- The gap **justifies** why your research is needed.

**Template answer:**
> "A literature review critically examines existing studies to understand the current state of knowledge. It helps identify the research gap — an area that has not been explored. For example, if many studies examine AI in education but none focus on rural schools, that is a research gap."

## 5. Common Abbreviations (Can appear as 5-mark)

| Abbreviation | Meaning | Use |
|---|---|---|
| i.e. | that is | to clarify |
| e.g. | for example | to give examples |
| et al. | and others | for citations with 3+ authors |
| etc. | and so on | to indicate more items |

**Key tip:** Don't use e.g. and etc. together — they are redundant.

---

## 6. Formal Email Format (Can appear as 5-mark)

**Structure:**
1. **To** + **Subject line** (clear and specific)
2. **Salutation** — Dear Sir/Madam
3. **Body** — Purpose → Details → Request
4. **Closing** — Yours sincerely
5. **Signature** — Name, Roll No., Department

**Just remember the flow:** *"Who → Why → What → Please → Thanks → Me"*
