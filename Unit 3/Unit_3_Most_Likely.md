## 1. Tense Usage (Most likely 5-mark or part of 10-mark)

**Just remember this table:**

| Section | Tense | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Introduction | Present | "AI is widely used." |
| Lit Review | Past | "Sharma (2022) found..." |
| Methodology | Past | "Data was collected..." |
| Results | Past | "Accuracy was 92%." |
| Conclusion | Present | "The model is effective." |

**Rule:** Present = facts/conclusions. Past = what was done/found.

---

## 2. Punctuation — Key Rules (Most likely 5-mark)

Memorize these 5 rules with examples:

1. **Comma** — separate list items: "Python, Java, and C++."
2. **Semicolon** — join related sentences: "The test passed; results were good."
3. **Colon** — before a list: "Tools used: Grammarly, QuillBot, ChatGPT."
4. **No contractions** — "do not" not "don't."
5. **Avoid comma splice** — ❌ "It worked, it was fast." ✅ "It worked; it was fast."

---

## 3. Common Errors — Memorize 5 with Examples (Most likely 5-mark or 10-mark)

| Error | ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|---|---|---|
| Subject-verb | "The data shows" | "The data show" |
| Comma splice | "It worked, it was fast" | "It worked; it was fast" |
| Dangling modifier | "Using Python, the accuracy improved" | "Using Python, we improved accuracy" |
| Wordiness | "due to the fact that" | "because" |
| Informal language | "really good" | "satisfactory" |

**Exam tip:** Every error you mention should have one wrong and one correct example.

## 4. AI Tools and Ethics (Most likely 10-mark or 5-mark)

**Tools:** ChatGPT (text generation), QuillBot (paraphrasing), Grammarly (grammar check).

**Benefits (memorize 3):**
1. Saves time
2. Improves grammar for non-native speakers
3. Helps with brainstorming and drafting

**Limitations (memorize 3):**
1. May produce inaccurate information
2. Cannot replace critical thinking
3. Students may become over-reliant

**Ethical concerns (memorize 3):**
1. Submitting AI text as your own = plagiarism
2. Not disclosing AI use = unethical
3. AI may change meaning while paraphrasing

**One-liner:** *"Use AI as a tool, not a replacement."*

---

## 5. Active vs Passive Voice (Can appear as 5-mark)

- **Active:** "We analyzed the data." → Clear, direct. Preferred.
- **Passive:** "The data was analyzed." → Focus on action. Used in methodology.

**Rule:** Use active voice mostly. Passive is OK in methods section.
