
## Unit I: Foundation of Technical and Scientific Writing

### Best 10-Mark Questions

1. Define technical writing. Explain its nature, scope, importance, and characteristics.
2. Explain various forms of scientific writing: thesis, dissertation, technical paper, manual, project report, and review article.
3. Explain the components of a thesis and the role of literature review, results, discussion, and conclusion.

### Best 5-Mark Questions

- Technical writing vs creative writing
- Abstract vs introduction
- Literature review and research gap
- Results vs discussion vs conclusion
- Features of a project report
- Common abbreviations: i.e., e.g., et al., etc.
- Formal email format

### Must Memorize

- Technical writing definition
- Thesis structure
- Literature review purpose
- Difference tables
- Email format

## Unit II: Writing Strategies and Research Communication

### Best 10-Mark Questions

1. Explain the structure, features, types, and purpose of technical reports.
2. Discuss research paper writing: structure, style, tone, referencing, and documentation.
3. Explain data collection, data analysis, graphic aids, editing, and proofreading in technical writing.

### Best 5-Mark Questions

- Descriptive abstract vs informative abstract
- Write a structured abstract with keywords
- Summary vs precis vs synopsis
- Executive summary and inverted pyramid style
- Graphic aids and their advantages
- APA/IEEE documentation style
- Citation vs reference
- Editing vs proofreading
- Plagiarism, paraphrasing, and patchwriting

### Must Practice

Write one structured abstract on a technical topic. This was directly asked in the mid-sem, so a similar practical question can return.

## Unit III: Grammar in Professional Writing

### Best 10-Mark Questions

1. Explain grammar in professional writing with reference to tense, punctuation, and sentence structure.
2. Discuss common errors in scientific writing with examples and corrections.
3. Explain the role, benefits, limitations, and ethical use of AI tools in writing and paraphrasing.

### Best 5-Mark Questions

- Tense usage in scientific writing
- Punctuation rules in technical writing
- Active voice vs passive voice
- Common errors with examples
- Technical and academic word usage
- AI tools such as ChatGPT and QuillBot
- Ethical concerns in AI paraphrasing

### Must Memorize

Examples. Grammar answers score better when every rule has a correct and incorrect example.

## Unit IV: Presentation Skills

### Best 10-Mark Questions

1. Explain how to plan and structure an effective technical presentation.
2. Discuss slide design, use of visual aids, voice modulation, and body language.
3. Explain how to handle Q&A, stage fear, and time management during presentations.

### Best 5-Mark Questions

- Structure of a presentation
- Principles of good slide design
- Use of visual aids
- Voice modulation
- Positive and negative body language
- Handling Q&A
- Stage fear and remedies
- Time management during presentation

### Must Prepare

Presentation questions are practical. Write answers as steps: before presentation, during presentation, after presentation/Q&A.

## Unit V: Professional and Interpersonal Communication

### Best 10-Mark Questions

1. Explain professional and interpersonal communication, including features, elements, and barriers.
2. Discuss group discussion: purpose, stages, skills tested, do's and don'ts, and feedback.
3. Explain technical talk, panel discussion, "Teach a Concept," and interview skills.

### Best 5-Mark Questions

- Professional communication features
- Barriers to communication
- Group discussion skills
- GD do's and don'ts
- Technical talk structure
- Panel discussion and role of moderator
- Teach a Concept method
- Interview preparation
- Common interview questions
- Listening skills and feedback

### Must Practice

Prepare one self-introduction, one GD opening, and one "Teach a Concept" example. These can be written quickly in the exam and look practical.
