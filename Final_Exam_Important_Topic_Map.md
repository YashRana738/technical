# 0. 100-Mark Final Exam Blueprint

Total marks: 100  
Unit distribution: 20 marks from each unit  
Likely question value: 5-mark and 10-mark questions

This means every unit is compulsory for preparation. Do not skip Units IV and V just because they were not in the mid-sem. In the final, each unit can independently contribute 20 marks.

## Possible Paper Pattern

| Per Unit Pattern | Meaning | Preparation Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| 2 x 10 marks | Two long answers from one unit | Prepare at least 2 strong long answers per unit |
| 1 x 10 + 2 x 5 marks | One long answer plus two medium answers | Prepare formats, comparisons, and explanations |
| 4 x 5 marks | Four medium questions | Prepare many short structured answers |
| Mixed subparts | One question split into 2+3+5 or 5+5 | Learn definitions, differences, examples, and uses |

## Best Strategy

For each unit, prepare:

- 2 long 10-mark answers
- 4 to 6 likely 5-mark answers
- 3 comparison tables
- 1 practical format or example where relevant

This gives enough coverage for either a 10+10 pattern or a 10+5+5 pattern.

## Unit II: Writing Strategies and Research Communication

### Best 10-Mark Questions

1. Explain the structure, features, types, and purpose of technical reports.
2. Discuss research paper writing: structure, style, tone, referencing, and documentation.
3. Explain data collection, data analysis, graphic aids, editing, and proofreading in technical writing.

### Best 5-Mark Questions

- Descriptive abstract vs informative abstract

### Must Practice

## Unit III: Grammar in Professional Writing

### Best 10-Mark Questions

1. Explain grammar in professional writing with reference to tense, punctuation, and sentence structure.
2. Discuss common errors in scientific writing with examples and corrections.
3. Explain the role, benefits, limitations, and ethical use of AI tools in writing and paraphrasing.

### Best 5-Mark Questions

- Tense usage in scientific writing
- Punctuation rules in technical writing
- Active voice vs passive voice
- Common errors with examples
- Technical and academic word usage
- AI tools such as ChatGPT and QuillBot
- Ethical concerns in AI paraphrasing

### Must Memorize

Examples. Grammar answers score better when every rule has a correct and incorrect example.

The examiner likes:

- Short, direct answers worth 2-4 marks.
- Differences in table form.
- "Why is it important?" questions.
- "Write a sample" questions.
- Technical writing ethics.
- Research/report structure.
- Applied communication, not just definitions.

For the final, expect the same style but now extended to Units IV and V.

# 3. Unit-Wise Exam Probability Map

## Unit I: Foundation of Technical and Scientific Writing

Most likely question forms:

1. Define technical writing and explain its nature and scope.
2. Differentiate technical writing and creative writing.
3. Explain various forms of scientific writing.
4. Describe the components of a thesis.
5. Differentiate abstract and introduction.
6. Explain literature review and its importance.
7. Differentiate results, discussion, and conclusion.
8. Write a formal technical email.
9. Explain common abbreviations used in technical writing.

High-scoring answer trick:

Use comparison tables and include examples. For thesis components, write in sequence.

## Unit II: Writing Strategies and Research Communication

Most likely question forms:

1. Write an abstract for a given technical topic.
2. Distinguish descriptive abstract and informative abstract.
3. Differentiate summary, precis, and synopsis.
4. Explain structure and features of technical reports.
5. Explain types and purposes of reports.
6. Explain graphic aids and why they are preferred over long text.
7. Discuss data collection and data analysis.
8. Explain inverted pyramid style in executive summaries.
9. Explain referencing/documentation styles and their importance.
10. Differentiate editing and proofreading.

High-scoring answer trick:

For abstract questions, use this formula:

Background + Objective + Method + Result/Expected Result + Conclusion + Keywords

## Unit III: Grammar in Professional Writing

Most likely question forms:

1. Explain tense usage in scientific writing with examples.
2. Explain common punctuation marks used in technical writing.
3. Discuss common errors in scientific writing.
4. Differentiate active and passive voice with examples.
5. Explain technical and academic word usage.
6. Discuss the role of AI tools in writing and paraphrasing.
7. What ethical issues arise while using AI writing tools?

High-scoring answer trick:

Always give examples. Grammar answers without examples look weak.

## Unit IV: Presentation Skills

Most likely question forms:

1. Explain how to plan and structure a presentation.
2. What are the principles of good slide design?
3. Explain the role of visual aids in presentation.
4. Discuss voice modulation and body language.
5. How should a presenter handle Q&A?
6. What is stage fear? How can it be overcome?
7. Explain time management in presentations.

High-scoring answer trick:

Write practical points, not only theory. Mention greeting, outline, slides, conclusion, Q&A, and timing.

## Unit V: Professional and Interpersonal Communication

Most likely question forms:

1. Define professional communication and explain its features.
2. Explain interpersonal communication and barriers.
3. What is group discussion? Explain skills tested in GD.
4. Write do's and don'ts of group discussion.
5. Explain technical talk and its structure.
6. What is panel discussion? Explain role of moderator.
7. Explain "Teach a Concept" with an example.
8. Discuss interview skills and preparation.
9. Write common interview questions and answer strategies.

High-scoring answer trick:

Use process-based answers: before, during, after. This works especially well for interviews, GD, presentations, and technical talks.

# 9. Final Exam Study Order

Because the paper is 100 marks with 20 marks from each unit, revise every unit. If time is limited, use this order inside each revision cycle:

1. First revise the 10-mark anchor topics from all five units.
2. Then revise 5-mark comparison questions from all five units.
3. Then practice formats: abstract, email, report structure, presentation structure, interview self-introduction.
4. Finally revise examples: grammar corrections, AI ethics, GD opening, Teach a Concept, and Q&A handling.

Recommended unit order for first full revision:

1. Unit I: technical writing, thesis, literature review, results-discussion-conclusion, email
2. Unit II: abstracts, reports, graphic aids, referencing, editing
3. Unit III: grammar, common errors, AI tools
4. Unit IV: presentation planning, slide design, voice/body language, Q&A, stage fear
5. Unit V: GD, panel discussion, teach a concept, interview skills

Reason: This follows the syllabus order and protects the equal 20-mark weight of every unit.
