# Unit IV: Presentation Skills — Most Likely / Very Little Time

> If you have very little time, study ONLY these topics from Unit IV. Structure your answers as: Before → During → After presentation.

---

## 1. Planning a Presentation (Most likely 10-mark)

**Three-phase structure — memorize this:**

**Before:**
- Know your audience and their knowledge level.
- Define one clear objective.
- Prepare slides: one idea per slide, large fonts, visuals.
- Practice with a timer.

**During:**
- Introduction (15%) → Body (70%) → Conclusion (15%).
- Use voice modulation — vary speed, volume, pitch.
- Maintain eye contact and open body language.
- Don't read from slides.

**After:**
- Summarize key points.
- Thank the audience.
- Handle Q&A calmly.

---

## 2. Slide Design (Most likely 5-mark)

**6 rules to memorize:**
1. One idea per slide
2. Bullet points, not paragraphs (max 6–8 lines)
3. Font size: Title 36+, Body 24+
4. High contrast (dark text on light background)
5. Consistent theme throughout
6. Use visuals (charts, images) over text

---

## 3. Body Language (Most likely 5-mark)

**DO ✅:**
- Eye contact
- Open hand gestures
- Upright posture
- Face the audience
- Smile

**DON'T ❌:**
- Cross arms
- Hands in pockets
- Fidget
- Read from slides
- Turn back to audience

---

## 4. Q&A Handling (Most likely 5-mark)

**6-step formula:**
1. Listen fully
2. Repeat the question
3. Answer concisely
4. Be honest if unsure — "I'll look into it"
5. Stay calm — don't argue
6. Thank questioners

---

## 5. Stage Fear (Most likely 5-mark)

**Causes:** Fear of judgment, lack of preparation, large audience.

**Top 5 remedies:**
1. Prepare thoroughly
2. Practice out loud multiple times
3. Deep breathing before going on stage
4. Positive self-talk — "I can do this"
5. Focus on the message, not yourself
