# Unit V: Professional and Interpersonal Communication — Most Likely / Very Little Time

## 1. Group Discussion (Most likely 10-mark)

**What is it?** A structured discussion among 6–10 people on a given topic, used in recruitment and evaluation.

**Skills tested:** Communication, leadership, teamwork, knowledge, listening, confidence.

**Do's ✅:** Speak clearly, listen to others, support with examples, stay on topic, be respectful.

**Don'ts ❌:** Don't shout, don't interrupt, don't dominate, don't stay silent, don't get personal.

**Stages:** Topic given → Think → Initiate → Discuss → Summarize → Conclude.

**Tip for exam:** Mention the stages, list 5 do's and 5 don'ts, and name the skills tested.

---

## 2. Panel Discussion + Role of Moderator (Most likely 5-mark)

**Panel Discussion** = Experts discuss a topic in front of an audience, guided by a moderator.

**Moderator's role (memorize 5):**
1. Introduces topic and panelists
2. Asks questions to guide discussion
3. Ensures everyone speaks (balanced participation)
4. Keeps discussion on track
5. Summarizes at the end + opens Q&A

---

## 3. Teach a Concept (Most likely 5-mark)

**Steps:**
1. Choose a simple concept
2. Define it in plain words
3. Use a real-life analogy
4. Break it down step by step
5. Summarize the key point

**Quick example — "Stack":**
> "A stack is like a pile of plates — you add and remove from the top. This is LIFO: Last In, First Out."

## 4. Interview Skills (Most likely 5-mark or part of 10-mark)

**Before:** Research company, know your resume, prepare common answers, dress well, arrive early.

**During:** Greet, eye contact, listen, answer concisely using STAR method, ask questions, thank.

**STAR Method:**
- **S**ituation — What was the context?
- **T**ask — What was your role?
- **A**ction — What did you do?
- **R**esult — What happened?

**Must-prepare questions:**
- Tell me about yourself (1-min intro: education + skills + goal)
- Strengths and weaknesses
- Why this role?

## 5. Barriers to Communication (Can appear as 5-mark)

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Physical | Noise, distance |
| Language | Jargon, different languages |
| Psychological | Stress, bias |
| Cultural | Different customs |
| Organizational | Hierarchy, info overload |

**Overcome:** Simple language + active listening + feedback + right channel.

---

## 6. Listening Skills and Feedback (Can appear as 5-mark)

**Listening tips:** Pay attention, don't interrupt, nod, ask questions, summarize.

**Feedback should be:** Specific, constructive, timely, balanced, actionable.
